Protein Priority

Aim for 1.4 – 2.0g of protein per kg of body weight to support muscle preservation and satiety.

Hydration Baseline

Drink 2.5 to 3.5 liters of clean water daily. Proper hydration optimizes cellular energy and digestion.

Micronutrients & Fiber

Incorporate at least 25-35g of prebiotic fiber from leafy greens, berries, seeds, and legumes.
